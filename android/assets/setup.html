Connect to your committee

This app opens your Staff Welfare Committee's records. Paste the app link the committee shared with you (on WhatsApp or by message).

How to paste: in WhatsApp, press and hold the link, choose Copy. Then press and hold in the box above and choose Paste.

You only do this once. To change it later, press the Back button on the app's first screen and choose Change link.
